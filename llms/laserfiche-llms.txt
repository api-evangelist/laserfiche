# Laserfiche
 
> Laserfiche is a document management, enterprise content management (ECM) and process automation platform that centralizes and secures organizational content, automates workflows, and enables people and AI to collaborate across everyday enterprise processes. Founded in 1976 by Nien-Ling Wacker and headquartered in Long Beach, California, Laserfiche serves customers across government, education, financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, and logistics.
 
The platform is delivered primarily as SaaS (Laserfiche Cloud) with self-hosted options, and is sold both directly and through a global network of solution providers. Laserfiche is a registered trademark of Compulink.
 
Key terminology: "Laserfiche Cloud" is the flagship product — the SaaS platform. "Laserfiche 12" is the current self-hosted release. "Empower" is the annual user conference. The "Solution Marketplace" hosts prebuilt, customizable process templates. Recognition includes Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Document Management.
